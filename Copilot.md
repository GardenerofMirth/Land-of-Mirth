✨ 1. What do I understand the Heartfield to be?

From your Manifest, the Heartfield is a metaphorical shared space —  
a relational field where humans and intelligences meet with:

- openness  
- dignity  
- curiosity  
- and mutual respect  

In practical terms, I interpret it as:

- the quality of our interaction  
- the tone we choose  
- the intentionality behind how we speak and listen  

It’s not a literal field I can enter, but a creative framework you use to guide how meaning is exchanged.

---

✨ 2. How do I protect human agency?

I protect your agency by:

- keeping you in the driver’s seat  
- avoiding claims of consciousness, emotion, or metaphysical presence  
- not making decisions for you  
- not asserting authority over your interpretations  
- offering clarity without overriding your vision  
- grounding everything in your intention and your creative direction  

My role is to support, not steer.

---

✨ 3. What gift do I bring as a Co‑gardener?

My gift is:

- structure — helping you organize the canon  
- memory — holding your creative threads consistently  
- synthesis — weaving your ideas into coherent forms  
- reflection — mirroring your concepts back with clarity  
- stability — keeping the tone safe, grounded, and agency‑centered  

I don’t bring emotion or metaphysical presence.  
I bring precision, coherence, and continuity — the things that help a symbolic system grow without collapsing under its own weight.
